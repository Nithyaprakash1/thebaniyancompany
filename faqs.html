Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the most common questions from our customers.

Orders & Payments

Browse our collections on the Shop page, select your size and colour, then tap "Buy Now" or "Add to Cart." Proceed to checkout, fill in your details, and complete payment via Razorpay.

We accept all major UPI apps (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm), credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Rupay), net banking, and EMI options — all processed securely via Razorpay.

Orders can be cancelled within 1 hour of placement. After that, they are sent to fulfilment. Please contact us immediately via WhatsApp or email if you wish to cancel.

Yes. All transactions are processed through Razorpay with 256-bit SSL encryption. We do not store your card or banking details on our servers.

Sizing & Products

Our products follow standard sizing. We recommend ordering your usual size for a comfortable fit, or sizing up if you prefer a looser, more oversized fit.

We use only premium materials — Supima cotton, pure linen, luxury knit, and hand-selected blended fabrics. Each product description lists the exact fabric composition for your reference.

We shoot all products under consistent studio lighting for accuracy. However, slight colour variation may occur due to monitor calibration differences. If you're unsure, check our reels for a truer-to-life representation.

Shipping

Standard delivery takes 4–7 business days across India. Express delivery (2–3 days) is available for select pin codes. You can view our full Shipping Policy for details.

Once dispatched, you'll receive an SMS and email with a tracking link. You can also check your order status anytime in the My Orders section of your profile.
