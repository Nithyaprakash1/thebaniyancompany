How to Initiate a Return

  1. 1 Go to My Orders in your profile and find the item you want to return.
  2. 2 Click "Request Return" and select a reason from the dropdown list.
  3. 3 Our team will confirm your request and schedule a pickup within 48 hours.
  4. 4 Refund or exchange will be processed within 5–7 business days after item is received.
